# Page taxonomy

- Compound tables identify structures through drawings, names, or notation. Record variable definitions and table continuation where present.
- Measurement tables bind experimental values to compound IDs. Preserve headers, conditions, qualifiers, and row ownership.
- Structure drawings include complete molecules, scaffolds, and substituents. Keep their roles and page evidence.
- Experimental prose can carry assay definitions, values, compound names, or references to figures and tables. Use it to resolve the evidence it explicitly supplies.
- A page can serve multiple extraction stages. Record unreadable regions separately from regions without relevant data.
